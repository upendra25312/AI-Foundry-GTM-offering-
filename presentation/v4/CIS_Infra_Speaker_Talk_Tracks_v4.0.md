**Slide 1. Cover**

Say: The answer first: we have five defined offerings and we ask you to choose which to test for 90 days. Our recommendation, on slide 4, is to start with the FinOps assessment and the AI gateway, because they are fastest to revenue and cheapest to prove. But the choice is yours.

Likely question: Why not launch all five now?

**Slide 4. Each option suits a goal; we would start with 04 and 03**

Say: Each offering serves a different goal: fast start, large deals, broad demand, differentiation or recurring revenue. If we had to choose, we would start with the FinOps assessment and the gateway, because they earn soonest with the least internal build effort, and they pull through the landing zone and managed operations. Leadership decides.

Likely question: Why not lead with agent governance? It is the most exciting.
Answer: It is, but its kill switch is unproven. We would fund its proof of concept in parallel and sell it once the evidence exists.
