# CIS Infra Azure AI portfolio v4.0: stakeholder objection guide

Prepared by Upendra Kumar, 11 Oct 2026. For each group: the likely objection, the prepared answer, and the slide that supports it. Deck references use the v4.0 slide numbers.

## Senior directors

| Objection | Prepared answer | Supporting slide |
|---|---|---|
| Why does CIS Infra need its own AI portfolio? | Agent Foundry, Neuro AI and the Microsoft Business Group build agents and apps; we supply the Azure platform, gateway, governance plumbing and operations underneath them. Boundaries are to confirm with each owner. | Deck 00 A7 |
| What is the number? | One customer buying all five is about $647k in year one and $540k a year after (illustrative rate). Portfolio size needs target accounts and attach rates from account leadership. | Deck 00 exec 7, A8 |
| What do we have to invest? | 15 to 70 role days of internal build per offering, plus lab and licence cash items that Finance sets. | Deck 00 A9 |
| What is our right to win? | Five testable claims, each with the evidence needed. None is used externally until proven. | Deck 00 exec 8 |
| Who has reviewed this? | Nobody yet. The sign-off plan is part of the 90-day validation. | Deck 00 A12 |
| How will it be governed? | P&L owner, three gates (validate, pilot, launch), monthly review, quarterly gate, stop rules. | Deck 00 A13, exec 10 |

## Security

| Objection | Prepared answer | Supporting slide |
|---|---|---|
